About, contact and privacy

Who runs the site

Cluefishing is run by Geir Amdal in Nordreisa, Norway. He is responsible for what is published here.

How to get in touch

Write to us through the site's public issue tracker: github.com/leamarketing/cluefishing/issues. You need a free GitHub account to post there, and what you write is public. Use it for corrections, a source we got wrong, or an accessibility problem.

How AI is used here

AI News: each edition is researched and drafted by an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) from the sources linked under every story. Geir Amdal reads and approves an edition before it goes live.

Story pictures: generated with an AI image tool, then cropped. Each edition says so.

The rest of the site: built and written with help from AI assistants, including Claude.

Privacy

The site has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no cookies. The Log form on the front page is a preview and sends nothing.

  • Saved in your own browser only: your language choice (cf_lang_choice) and your answers in the Tackle Box checklist (cluefishing_tacklebox_v1). They are never sent anywhere. You can delete them by clearing site data for cluefishing.com in your browser.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores the IP address of visitors for security purposes. See GitHub's note on data collection.
  • Fonts: most pages still load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page opens. We are moving the fonts onto this site so that stops.
  • Links: the source links in AI News go to other sites, which have their own privacy rules.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA on every page. If something is hard to read or use, tell us through the issue tracker above.
