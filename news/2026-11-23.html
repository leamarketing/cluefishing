Google widens its visible AI label on ads to California, South Korea, and Vietnam

Google Ads already showed a visible "AI created" or "AI edited" label directly on ads that use AI-generated images or video in the EU, India, and New York. This month Google extended that same on-ad label to California, South Korea, and Vietnam, so people in six places can now see at a glance when an ad they're looking at was made or touched up with AI.

Paris Hilton and AOC return to Capitol Hill to push the Defiance Act over the finish line

Paris Hilton joined Reps. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Laurel Lee on Capitol Hill this week to urge the House to finally vote on the bipartisan Defiance Act, which would let people sue anyone who knowingly creates or shares sexually explicit deepfakes of them. The Senate has already passed it unanimously, twice, and Ocasio-Cortez says talks with the Speaker's office about scheduling a vote have been encouraging.

The European Commission confirms it was hit by an AI-generated cyberattack, and says it's back online

The European Commission confirmed this week that a cybercampaign it detected in October was built using a publicly available AI tool, the first time the EU has reported a cyberattack made with AI. A spokesperson said the affected systems were taken offline as a precaution and have since been restored, with no evidence so far of a wider data breach.
