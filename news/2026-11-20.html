Google widens its "made or edited with AI" ad label to California, South Korea, and Vietnam

Google has extended the visible overlay that marks ads built or significantly edited with AI, previously shown only in the EU, India, and New York, to three more places, with the change rolling out through November for creatives bought via Google Ads and Display & Video 360. It's a small, concrete step in the direction this site cares about: showing people plainly when an ad leaned on generated content, instead of leaving them to guess.

Fact-checkers catch three AI-made videos putting false words in Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's mouth

The watchdog group NewsGuard identified three AI-generated videos, traced to accounts linked to China, that used a cloned voice to put fabricated statements in Jensen Huang's mouth; an AI voice detector it used rated the audio as 99% likely to be synthetic. It's a useful reminder that the same AI detection tools this site champions are already catching impersonations like this quickly, before they can spread unchecked.

Source: washingtontimes.com →

MIT's new AI "copilot" pauses mid-design so engineers can weigh in before a structure's shape is locked in

MIT researchers built a tool for topology optimization, the process of shaping structural parts, that stops partway through and shows a human designer the regions it's still deciding on, rather than only presenting a finished result. In the team's tests, the collaboration produced structures with higher buckling strength that were also easier to manufacture, adding only about 15 seconds to the total design time.
