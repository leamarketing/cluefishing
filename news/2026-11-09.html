An MIT model roughly doubles how far ahead weather forecasts can see

A team including MIT's Judah Cohen built a machine-learning layer that corrects the errors in existing forecasting models for the tricky two-to-six-week range, roughly doubling their accuracy there and, for the first time, beating long-term climatological averages in that window. It topped the field in ECMWF's AI WeatherQuest competition, and because it sits on top of any underlying forecast rather than replacing it, the gain is a real one for farmers, utilities and anyone planning around extreme weather.

A chatbot that talks to patients before they see the doctor just had its first real clinical trial

In a feasibility study published in The Lancet, 98 patients at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center chatted with Google's research AI, AMIE, before an urgent primary-care visit, with a physician watching every conversation in real time. No chat needed a safety stop, and AMIE's list of possible diagnoses included the doctor's eventual call nine times out of ten, though physicians still did better at turning that into a practical, affordable plan.

Microsoft's AI "panel of doctors" outdid generalist physicians on hundreds of hard diagnostic cases

In a peer-reviewed study in Science, Microsoft's AI Diagnostic Orchestrator worked through 304 of medicine's toughest published case reports by running several AI models as a virtual panel that proposes diagnoses and orders tests round by round. It correctly solved 83% of cases against 36% for generalist physicians who could consult references. The comparison used retrospective case write-ups rather than real patients, and Microsoft's own next step is a prospective hospital study to see if that holds up.
