TikTok takes its deepfake checker worldwide, and opens it up for anyone to use

TikTok told its Safety and Innovation Forum in London that Likeness Detection, the tool that lets creators and public figures find AI deepfakes using their face, is moving beyond its original US test into a global rollout. The company also opened a free public checker that lets anyone see whether a photo or video was made or edited with TikTok's own AI tools, alongside a new milestone: more than five billion AI-generated videos labelled on the platform so far.

A new Philippine bill would require clear AI labels and consent before anyone makes a deepfake of you

Senator Mark Villar filed the "Kontra Malisyosong AI Act" in the Philippine Senate this week, which would require a visible label on AI-generated or AI-altered content shared publicly and written or digital consent before someone makes a deepfake of a real person in a way likely to cause harm. It would also give platforms 24 hours to act once a violation is reported, with fines for a missing or tampered label. The bill still needs committee hearings, three Senate readings, a House counterpart, and the President's signature.

An AI tool just became the first cleared to read five-year breast cancer risk from a mammogram you already had

The FDA cleared DeepHealth's Mammo Risk, which the company says is the first AI tool cleared to estimate someone's three, four, and five-year breast cancer risk from mammogram images a clinic already has on file, with no extra scan needed. The clearance rested on an independent study of more than 65,000 screening mammograms across 10 US clinics, where DeepHealth reports the tool held up well for women with dense breast tissue and for Black women, two groups current risk models often serve less accurately.
