Anthropic opens its AI-for-science program to 20 research foundations and institutions

Free Claude access, which had mostly gone to individual researchers, is now reaching organizations including the Simons Foundation, the US National Science Foundation, the Wellcome Sanger Institute and the Francis Crick Institute, who can pass it on across hundreds of projects in biology, medicine, physics, chemistry, climate science and mathematics.

Source: anthropic.com →

Google DeepMind's Genie 4 generates interactive 3D worlds with real-time sound to match

The new world model adds ambient noise, music and even dialogue to the scenes it builds as you explore them, and lets you reshape a scene mid-simulation, from changing the weather to adding new objects. It's still a limited research preview, and Google says keeping a scene consistent much past about two minutes remains an open problem.

Source: northeasttimes.com →

Mistral hands over the keys to a trillion-parameter open model built for defenders

France's Mistral released the full downloadable weights for Large 4, letting any security team run and inspect a frontier-scale model built to help find and patch vulnerabilities, on as few as four GPUs. The company itself has cautioned that the same openness means a downloaded copy can't be recalled if someone repurposes it for attacks instead.
