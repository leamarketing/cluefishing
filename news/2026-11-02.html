Anthropic opens its AI-for-science program to 20 foundations, institutions and research groups

Free Claude credits, which began with individual researchers, now also reach 20 foundations, institutions and research groups, including the Simons Foundation, the US National Science Foundation, the Wellcome Sanger Institute and the Francis Crick Institute. The wider program has already supported hundreds of projects in biology, medicine, physics, chemistry, climate science and mathematics.

Source: anthropic.com →

Google DeepMind's Genie 4 generates interactive 3D worlds with real-time sound to match

The new world model adds ambient sound, music and dialogue to the scenes it builds as you explore them, and lets you reshape a scene mid-simulation, from changing the weather to adding new objects. It is still a limited research preview, and DeepMind says keeping a scene consistent for more than a few minutes remains an open problem.

Source: deepmind.google →

Mistral hands over the keys to a trillion-parameter open model built for defenders

France's Mistral released the downloadable weights for Large 4, its trillion-parameter model, positioned for defensive cybersecurity such as finding and patching vulnerabilities. In the same announcement Mistral says that, although the model refused black-box attacks on live targets, it still sees a potential for the open weights to help carry out real attacks, and it is changing the guardrails on the service it controls.
